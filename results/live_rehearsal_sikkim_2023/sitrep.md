## Observations and uncertainty

- Radar change, optical change and water predictions are restricted to low ground near rivers. Mountain detections must connect to a river channel, whereas weaker change and water predictions with less support are assigned to the possible class. 0.1 km² of the likely extent could not be observed through cloud; the extent there is inferred from the flood level estimated along the same river reach and is excluded from strong road-break evidence.
- Road segments are at most 50 m long. A segment is classified as likely impassable when at least half of it, or a continuous 50 m length, lies within the likely flood extent. Strong road-break evidence requires optical change persisting in later images, radar change on two orbits, or overlapping radar and optical change. The radar water model uses the same images as radar change and is counted with that radar evidence.
- The most recent images are from 15 Nov 2023. Cloud and radar shadow prevent observation of 1.3 km², and repairs or new damage after this date cannot be assessed. Buildings and roads absent from pre-event OpenStreetMap are omitted. Road status is calculated from overlap with the extent; bridges are assessed from their approaches.
